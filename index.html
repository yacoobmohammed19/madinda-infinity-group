Who we are

Capable people. Safer workplaces.

Established in 2022, Madinda Infinity Group provides training, personal protective equipment (PPE) supplies and consulting services to businesses, organisations and individuals.

Our focus is on developing practical skills, supporting business needs and supplying protective equipment suited to our clients' working environments. We serve clients in all provinces and cities across South Africa, and we are currently working towards accreditation for selected training programmes.

Our missionTo equip people and organisations with practical skills, the right protective equipment and dependable advice, so they can work safely, perform better and grow.
